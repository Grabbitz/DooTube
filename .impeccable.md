## Design Context

### Users and Usage
- The creator wants a Rust-native macOS app for watching YouTube and listening to YouTube Music, using the installed Kaset as a reference rather than a requirement to copy its interface.
- Stated goals: smoother YouTube use, continued compatibility with YouTube changes, and deeper Music information and customization. These are goals, not measured performance results or verified provider capabilities.
- Broader audience segments, listening environments, and detailed user workflows remain proposals in the [design brief](docs/design-brief.md), not validated user research.

### Confirmed Direction and Priority
- **Latest confirmed direction:** the creator moved beyond the earlier “เรียบแบบ mac” direction, selected Cinema / Music, and requested a striking Netflix-inspired interface. After approving the standalone preview, the creator explicitly approved its integration into the real Rust/AppKit app; Cinema 0.3.0 is now installed locally. This does not approve replacing the official players or delivering every proposed feature.
- **Confirmed product priority:** “เอา youtube ให้ลื่นแล้วไปเน้นmusic” — make YouTube smooth first, then prioritize Music depth and customization.
- Delivery interpretation: prove permitted playback first, deliver a smooth Watch daily core, then shift the main focus to Music. Keep early Music/EQ feasibility limited to exposing architectural and policy blockers; completing all Music exploration is not a prerequisite for Watch development.
- The Cinema integration approval does not extend to unimplemented APIs, architecture changes, or every proposed feature. Existing capability, policy, permission, and DRM constraints in [Architecture](docs/architecture.md) still apply.

### Personality and Aesthetic Direction
- **Current implementation interpretation, not approved brand copy:** cinematic, immersive, media-first.
- Retain straightforward daily interactions and progressive disclosure of advanced controls; the approved Cinema integration is no longer constrained to the earlier Mac-like visual direction.
- **Implemented production styling:** `resources/cinema/tokens.css` defines warm near-black OKLCH surfaces (`13% 0.008 27`, with raised surfaces at 19% and 27%), warm off-white ink (`96% 0.005 65`), and a red accent (`64% 0.2 27`). Cinema Home uses locally bundled SIL-OFL Bebas Neue for display type and a native-system body stack; the native Cinema bar uses AppKit fonts and matching sRGB palette projections. These describe 0.3.0, not broader brand/default approvals. Root `tokens.css` remains preview-only.
- Wider branding, density, inspector defaults, and future styling decisions remain proposals. No minimum macOS version has been approved; the installed reference app's minimum is not DooTube's requirement.

### Design Principles
1. Keep daily watching simple; reveal advanced tools when requested. Future layouts and settings beyond the implemented Cinema integration remain proposals.
2. Prove playback and the Watch daily core before making Music depth/customization the delivery focus; investigate only enough Music/EQ feasibility early to avoid architectural dead ends.
3. Preserve playback context and queue through navigation and recoverable errors, within the provider's permitted lifecycle. Current behavior is recorded in [Implementation](docs/implementation.md); unimplemented workflow details and transition acceptance criteria in the design brief remain proposals.
4. Treat smoothness as something to measure against the installed Kaset on the same hardware, network, media, and access conditions, including WebKit helper costs—not as a benefit guaranteed by Rust or an already-achieved result. Numeric budgets are not user-approved.
5. Show only capabilities and media information that can actually be supported and substantiated; do not hide required official player UI or bypass provider restrictions.
